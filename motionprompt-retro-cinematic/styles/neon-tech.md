# Neon tech

**One-liner:** a deep purple-to-violet background with a hot magenta glow from one edge, fine screen lines and a dark vignette; big faint HUD rings draw on with a fast spin then turn slowly; thin circuit traces with glowing pulses run in from the edges; an isometric violet laptop lands, its lid swings open and the cyan screen flickers on; holographic panels rise out of the screen one by one, each scanning in with a quick glitch and linked back by glowing data lines; badges pop; the title decodes letter by letter, the key word slams in huge in magenta with a glow flicker and colour split, and an outline pill draws itself under it with the tagline; a soft scan line sweeps now and then; it ends with a short glitch-out.
**Best for:** tech, AI, cloud, security and developer products, launch or feature slides, brand intros with a futuristic feel.
**Avoid when:** you need a calm or warm tone.

## Inputs to gather
- Title first line (white) and the key word (magenta, huge), tagline (short). Topic icons for 3 to 4 panels (cloud, lock, chart, code, mail, brain for AI). Device (laptop by default). Brand colours.

## Build recipe
- **Rings:** 3 concentric SVG groups (solid thin, dashed, tick ring); `rotation` from a fast spin (720 degrees over 0.6 s, `power3.out`) into a slow constant `t * 4` degrees per second; arcs drawn with `strokeDashoffset`.
- **Traces:** generate 8 to 10 polylines from the frame edges toward the centre with right-angle bends (seeded), round nodes at the corners; pulses = a short bright dash whose `strokeDashoffset` advances with `t` (finite cycles).
- **Panels:** rounded rects with cyan strokes and low-alpha fills; `y 40 to 0` + an inner scan bar; glitch = two stepped frames offsetting a red and a cyan copy by +-4 px; icons as small inline SVG paths.
- **Decoding text:** for each letter, before its lock time show `chars[floor(hash(frameIndex + i) * chars.length)]`; at lock time show the true letter with a brief brighter colour; keep the width fixed with a monospace-like tracking or fixed-width spans.
- **Slam:** the key word starts at scale 2.2 and opacity 0 then `expo.out`, glow via layered text-shadow (magenta), flicker = 3 stepped alpha changes within 0.25 s; colour split = red/cyan copies at +-3 px for 0.15 s.
- **Pitfalls:** avoid strong strobing (the flicker changes brightness less than 15%); keep text on the safe area; the fine lines overlay must not lower text contrast.

## Sound plan
How each effect of the brief is covered:
- **A rising power-up as the rings spin on:** `riser` (tonal) at `rings`.
- **A soft thunk as the laptop lands, a small motor whir as the lid opens and three bleeps as the screen turns on:** `thud` + `whir` + `run` of three `blip` at `laptop`.
- **A quick scan hiss and a run of data chirps for each panel:** `hiss` + `ticks` of `chirp`/`blip` at `panel*`.
- **Tiny ticks while the title decodes with a brighter blip as each letter locks:** `typing` + `blip` at `decode`.
- **A big synth zap with a sub thump when the key word slams in, and an electric buzz in time with its flicker:** `zap` + `impact` + `buzz` at `slam`.
- **A soft rising tone as the pill draws:** `chirp` at `pill`.
- **Bubbly pops for the badges:** `pop` at `badge*`.
- **Airy sweeps for the scan lines:** `whoosh` at `scan`.
- **A quiet electric hum under it all and a glitchy stutter at the end:** `hum` bed, `glitch` at `glitchout`.

## Layout by aspect ratio
- **16:9:** title left-centre, laptop and panels right; rings behind the title.
- **9:16 / 4:5:** title in the upper third (key word large below the first line), laptop and panels in the middle-lower band, badges around; keep pill and tagline above the safe zone.
- **1:1:** title top, laptop bottom-right.

## Loop & ending
A short glitch-out cuts to the empty violet background with only the rings faintly turning (frame 0).

## Guardrails
- Keep every line of text short and easy to read in a second.
- Glow flicker and glitch are brief and mild: no strobing.
- Panels and icons match the topic; do not invent product claims.

## QA
- Frame at `panel3 + 0.5 s`: three panels visible with icons, connector lines drawing.
- Frame at `decode + 0.6 s`: some letters locked, others still random; at `slam + 0.4 s`: key word huge in magenta with a slight split.
- Frame at `pill + 0.6 s`: pill outline complete with the tagline inside.
